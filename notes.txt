Research! With pictures & video
===============================
ICTAI 2026
Scalable Multi-robot Coverage with Connectivity Maintenance Via Deep Reinforcement Learning

Jacomo Corrieri  ·  Iliya Kulbaka  ·  Anirban Ghosh  ·  Ayan Dutta

First author publication

My first every publication, this research studies the feasibility of reinforcement learning for multi-robot area coverage 
with communication constraints -- the robots must remain in a connected team formation throughout exploration. As first author,
I led the engineering effort of creating an end-to-end experimentation framework: the simulation environment, map and obstacle generation,
PyTorch neural network models, reward functions, and customizable parameters -- each component being modular to allow for quick iterations. 
In our approach, agents leverage convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to process multi-channel observations of the world and team state.
During training, a centralized critic guides agent decisions, but during evaluation, agents are limited to their own partial views, 
a methodology known as Centralized Training, Decentralized Execution (CTDE). 

Key Contributions:
* Simulated up to 50 autonomous robots with Python and Gymnasium, achieving 5x more scalability than prior work.
* Developed a decentralized CNN model with PyTorch, resulting in 15% greater area coverage compared to our baseline.
* Visualized performance metrics with Matplotlib and TensorBoard for data analysis and model evaluations.
